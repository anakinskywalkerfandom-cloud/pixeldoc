🛡️ Your images are converted entirely on your device

The JPG to PDF conversion process runs entirely in your browser using PDF-lib, an open-source JavaScript library for creating PDF documents. When you add image files, your browser reads them from your local storage into memory. PDF-lib then constructs the PDF data structure in JavaScript, embedding each image into the appropriate page. The resulting PDF is written directly to a file download without any intermediate step that touches a server or network connection.

This means your photographs, scanned documents and other images are never transmitted to any party. Sensitive documents such as identity documents, financial records, medical images and personal photographs can be converted without any privacy concern. Once you close the browser tab, all data from the session is released from memory.
